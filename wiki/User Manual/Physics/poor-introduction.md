let's start with big things: Celestial bodies

Big bodies have a big mass, and so give a lot of gravity.  
Gravity is a force, a push (it's what keeps you to the ground\! and makes airplanes pricey) and in cosmonautics we want to cancel it all out with thrust, or lift, but lift requires air and also gives out drag.  
Thrust is produced by rocket engines (without air) or by propellers (with air).  
If you're never going to leave the atmosphere (and don't go to supersonic speeds) a propeller is recommended. If you're going over the atmosphere, a rocket engine is REQUIRED because a propeller won't work in the vacuum of space.
